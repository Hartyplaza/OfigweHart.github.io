Customer Loyalty Prediction in Banking

This project compares AI models and PLS-SEM (Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling) to predict and explain customer loyalty in the banking sector. Using a dataset of demographics, satisfaction, trust, and behavioral indicators, the study explored both predictive accuracy and theoretical insights. The workflow included data preprocessing, feature grouping, model training (Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Networks, PLS Regression), and PLS-SEM structural modeling.
Result: PLS Regression achieved the best predictive performance, while PLS-SEM confirmed key loyalty drivers such as trust and satisfaction. A hybrid framework combining AI and PLS-SEM was recommended for both prediction and interpretation..

Modeling Non-Stationarity and Cointegration with ECM

This project analyzes GOOGL and MSFT stock prices to explore non-stationarity, spurious regression, and cointegration in financial time series. It demonstrates how cointegration reveals a long-run equilibrium and builds an Error Correction Model (ECM) to capture both short-run dynamics and long-run adjustments.
Result: The ECM successfully modeled the equilibrium relationship between the two stocks, highlighting short-term deviations and their correction toward the long-run path.
